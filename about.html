What We’re About

Our website is all about the French Revolution — a time when the people of France stood up for freedom, equality, and change. Tired of unfair taxes and powerful rulers, ordinary citizens challenged the monarchy and reshaped their nation. Through rebellion, reform, and courage, the French Revolution sparked ideas that still influence the world today.

Key Figures

  • King Louis XVI: The last king before the Revolution.
  • Marie Antoinette: The queen known for her lavish spending.
  • Maximilien Robespierre: A revolutionary leader during the Reign of Terror.
  • Napoleon Bonaparte: Took control of France after the Revolution ended.

Why It Matters Today

The French Revolution taught the world that people have the power to demand fairness, equality, and change. It proved that ordinary citizens could stand up against unfair rulers and create a government that listens to the people. The Revolution’s ideas about freedom, democracy, and human rights inspired movements all around the world, from Europe to the Americas. Even today, we see its influence in the belief that everyone deserves a voice in their government and equal treatment under the law. The courage and determination of the French people continue to remind us that change is possible when people unite for justice.
